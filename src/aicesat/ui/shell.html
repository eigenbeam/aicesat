Cross-mission altimetry

Compare ground- and ice-surface elevations from three NASA missions over the same place, across time: ICESat-1 (2003–09), IceBridge (2009–19), and ICESat-2 (2018–).

How it works

  1. Coverage — the globe: which missions measured each hex, and when. Nothing is fetched to show it.
  2. Change — click a hex: every mission's measurements there, and each small cell coloured by how its surface changed. Click a cell for its 20-year record.
  3. Study — shift-click cells, then Study: ICESat-2 photons in 3-D and the height difference between missions (Δh).
Co-registration aligns two missions to a common frame and time before comparing. Δh is the height difference at the same spot. Hover the ? next to any control for more.
